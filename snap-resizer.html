Snapchat stories are 1080 × 1920 (9:16). Anything below 720 px wide gets upscaled by Snapchat and looks soft — that's the floor. Cover scales up and centre-crops to fill the frame (best for full-bleed stories). Contain fits the whole image and pads the rest. Stretch forces the dimensions. Tip: keep captions ~150 px clear of the top and bottom, where Snapchat's UI sits.
